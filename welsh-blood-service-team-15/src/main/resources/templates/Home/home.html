What we do

At WelshBlood, we are dedicated to saving lives and supporting our community by ensuring a steady and reliable blood supply. Here's how we make an impact:

  • Collecting Blood Donations: We organize regular blood drives and maintain donor centers where individuals can contribute blood to help those in need.
  • Blood Processing and Storage: Our expert team processes and stores blood donations to ensure they meet safety and quality standards, making them available for hospitals and clinics.
  • Matching and Distribution: We coordinate with medical facilities to ensure that donated blood is efficiently matched and distributed to patients who require it most.
